# Log hours and invoice clients from your AI chat

Tell {{PRODUCT}} what you worked on, and it logs the hours to the right client. Ask what's unbilled, and it drafts the invoice. No timer, no spreadsheet, no separate app to open.

- "Log 2 hours on the Acme redesign"
- "Reconstruct my Tuesday from my calendar and commits"
- "Invoice Acme for last month and send it"

## Why it's different

**No timer to remember.** Just tell it what you did, whenever you remember to — this morning, at lunch, or at the end of the day. No start/stop button to forget.

**Reconstruct a week from your calendar and commits.** Forgot to log four days? Point it at your calendar events and git history and it proposes entries mapped to your projects, flags gaps and overlaps, and asks before logging anything uncertain.

**Invoice with a Stripe payment link in one message.** It drafts the invoice from your unbilled hours, shows you the total before anything is created, and only emails it — with a payment link your client can pay instantly — once you say to send it.
